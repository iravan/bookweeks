## Description (up to 4000 characters)

**Book Weeks - Your Complete Class Management Solution**

Book Weeks is the perfect companion for educators, tutors, coaches, and anyone who manages weekly classes or sessions. Streamline your scheduling, track student attendance, and stay organized with an intuitive calendar-based interface.

**Key Features:**

📅 **Smart Week Calendar**
- View 4 weeks at a glance with a clean, easy-to-read calendar
- Visual indicators show which days have scheduled sessions
- Quick navigation between weeks
- Automatically highlights the current week

📚 **Session Management**
- Create and edit class sessions with custom titles and descriptions
- Set start and end times for each session
- Organize sessions by day with scrollable tabs
- View all events in a comprehensive list with search functionality

👥 **Student Management**
- Add students with detailed information (name, nickname, age, phone number)
- Assign students to specific sessions
- Track attendance and participation
- Edit student information anytime
- View all registered students in one place

🎯 **Template System**
- Save frequently used sessions as templates
- Quickly create new sessions from templates
- Include student lists in templates for recurring classes
- Manage and edit templates easily

📊 **Data Management**
- Export all your data to YAML files for backup
- Import data to restore or transfer between devices
- All data is stored securely on your device

✨ **User-Friendly Design**
- Beautiful, modern interface with custom color scheme
- Intuitive navigation and smooth interactions
- Optimized for all iOS devices
- Safe area support for all screen sizes

**Perfect For:**
- Private tutors managing multiple students
- Coaches organizing training sessions
- Music teachers scheduling lessons
- Language instructors tracking classes
- Any educator managing weekly schedules

**Why Book Weeks?**

Book Weeks simplifies your class management workflow. No more juggling multiple calendars or spreadsheets. Everything you need is in one place - your schedule, your students, and your sessions, all beautifully organized and easy to access.

Start organizing your teaching schedule today with Book Weeks!
